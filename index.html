03 / Monograph The data sheet

A full workup.

BPC-157
CAS 137525-51-0
MW 1419.55 g/mol
SEQ Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val
RES 15-mer · pentadecapeptide
// Mechanism

BPC-157 is a partial sequence of body protection compound (BPC) isolated from gastric juice. It accelerates angiogenesis via VEGFR-2 upregulation, modulates the NO-system, and increases growth-hormone receptor expression on tendon fibroblasts — meaning it routes healing signals directly to soft tissue under load.

// Researched effects

Tendon & ligament repair · gastric ulcer healing · gut barrier integrity · neuroprotection · vascular regeneration · counteracts NSAID-induced damage in animal models.

// Notes

Research-chemical status. Not FDA-approved for human use. Stable to gastric acid (oral bioavailability remains under investigation; subcutaneous is the studied route). Always consult a physician.
